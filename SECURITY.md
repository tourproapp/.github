# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in one of our repositories, please report it privately:

- Email [security@tourpro.app](mailto:security@tourpro.app) (or [info@tourpro.app](mailto:info@tourpro.app))
- Or use "Report a vulnerability" on the repository's **Security** tab

Please include steps to reproduce, and avoid accessing or modifying other users' data.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days, and we credit researchers who follow coordinated disclosure. Our disclosure coordinates are also published at [tourpro.app/.well-known/security.txt](https://tourpro.app/.well-known/security.txt).

## Scope

This policy applies to all repositories in the tourproapp organization unless a repository states otherwise.
